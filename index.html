THE SNMB STORY

SNMB

Samahan ng mga Baliw

We honestly don't remember how it all started or why we chose this name. Somehow, we just clicked.

With us, even the most random and pointless things can turn into a good time. We don't always need a plan or a special occasion. Sometimes, all it takes is being in the same place, laughing at things that probably make no sense to anyone else.

Somewhere along the way, our circle became a kind of home. A place where we can be ourselves, find comfort in one another, and enjoy the little things without having to make them mean something bigger.

Maybe that's what makes this friendship special. It's not about doing something amazing every day. It's about having people who make ordinary moments feel good just because you're sharing them.
